Ritual design system

Quiet structure for a personal operating system.

A compact desktop language built from warm paper surfaces, graphite type, native restraint, and semantic color. Hierarchy comes from spacing and tone before decoration.

Provisional baselineUpdated 2026-07-15Light + dark4px base unit

Character

Ritual should feel stable, private, and quietly capable—a desktop instrument that recedes while the user’s work stays prominent.

01 / Quiet

Neutral by default

Warm whites and restrained graphite establish hierarchy without turning every section into a card or every action into color.

02 / Compact

Dense, not cramped

Thirty-pixel rows and short controls support desktop efficiency while consistent groups preserve breathing room.

03 / Semantic

Meaning over decoration

Teal, green, amber, and red communicate focus or state. They are punctuation, never ambient decoration.
