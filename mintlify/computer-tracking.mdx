---
title: "Computer Tracking"
description: "How Ritual's background Rust watcher captures your computer activity."
---

Ritual's computer tracking is powered by a Rust process built into the Tauri desktop app. It runs silently in the background and gives you an automatic record of how you spend your time on your computer — without any manual logging.

## What gets tracked

| Signal | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Active app | The foreground application at each polling interval |
| Window title | The active window title (useful for identifying tasks within an app) |
| Browser domains | Domains visited, aggregated into daily summaries |
| AFK time | Periods of inactivity — excluded from active time calculations |
| Device info | Machine name and platform, used for multi-device support |

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Device registration">
    On first launch, your machine registers itself with the backend. This supports multiple devices under one account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Event recording">
    The watcher polls at a configurable interval, records the active app and window title, and writes events to local SQLite.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Daily rollups">
    At the end of each day (and on demand), the watcher computes aggregated summaries: total time per app, top browser domains, AFK percentage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Projection to habit log">
    The `Computer Use` habit is automatically updated based on your total active computer time for the day. No manual logging needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cloud sync">
    Events sync to Tinybird asynchronously. This powers the analytics views and lets the AI assistant answer questions about your computer usage over long time windows.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Daily summaries

The watcher generates several daily summary types you can query from the dashboard:

- **Top apps** — which apps you used most, ranked by time
- **Browser summary** — domain breakdown for your browser usage
- **AFK summary** — how much of your logged time was actually active
- **Domain breakdown** — daily breakdown per browser domain

## Privacy and exclusions

You have full control over what gets tracked. From **Settings → Computer Tracking → Exclusions**, you can:

- Add specific apps to the exclusion list
- Remove apps from exclusions
- See Ritual's suggested exclusions based on your usage patterns

<Info>
  Computer activity data is stored locally in SQLite on your machine first. Cloud sync to Tinybird is opt-in and happens in the background. You can disable it entirely in settings.
</Info>

## Screen recording search

If you have screen recording enabled, Ritual indexes your recordings locally. The `local_search_bridge.rs` Rust module connects this local index to Typesense, so you can search your activity history with natural language — useful for reconstructing what you were working on at a specific time.
