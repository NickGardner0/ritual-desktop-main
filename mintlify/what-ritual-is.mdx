Ritual is a personal tracking app built to help you see what is actually happening in your life. It brings habits, passive sensor data, and context together into one place so your daily review becomes simple.

## The problem with existing tools

Most tracking apps are built around one of two flawed models:

- **Streaks apps** — gamify logging but don't help you understand patterns
- **Quantified self dashboards** — show you data but don't connect it to behavior

Ritual takes a different approach. It treats your data as a unified picture of your day — integrating wearable signals, computer activity, and manual logs — so that when you sit down to review your day, the context is already there.

## What it captures

Ritual collects data across four categories:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Wearable signals" icon="heart-pulse">
    Sleep duration, recovery score, and daily strain from Whoop. Activity and heart rate from Apple Watch via the iOS companion app.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Computer activity" icon="desktop">
    App usage, window titles, browser domains, and AFK time — tracked passively by the Rust watcher process running in the background.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Manual habits" icon="pen">
    Any habit you define: workouts, reading, meditation, caffeine, water intake. Logged with a single tap. Smart synonym matching means natural language works.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Context" icon="cloud-sun">
    Daily weather is automatically added to every log entry, giving you environmental context without any extra work.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## The philosophy

<Info>
  One day I'm sure everyone will routinely collect all sorts of data about themselves and their behavior. But because I've been interested in personal science for a long time, I started extensively self-tracking — and found that the tooling to make that data actually useful didn't exist. Ritual is the attempt to build it.
</Info>

The goal isn't to optimize a single metric. It's to build a personal model of your life — one where habits, learning, time, and trade-offs are treated as native primitives, not afterthoughts bolted onto a general-purpose productivity tool.

## Who it's for

Ritual is built for people who:

- Already self-track but feel overwhelmed by scattered apps and disconnected data
- Want to understand the *interplay* between sleep, focus, habits, and time — not just individual metrics
- Care more about long-term patterns than real-time dashboards
- Want a calm, 5-minute daily review rather than a constant stream of notifications
