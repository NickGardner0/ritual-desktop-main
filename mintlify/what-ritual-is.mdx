Ritual is a personal tracking app built to help you see what is actually happening in your life.

It brings habits, computer activity, phone usage, health signals, location context, and trends into one place so your daily review becomes simple.

## The Problem

Most people track things — steps, sleep, screen time, workouts, focus, caffeine, location, and app usage — but never actually use that data. It sits in separate apps, in disconnected silos, and never adds up to anything meaningful.

Ritual is different. It treats your data as a unified picture of your day, not a collection of isolated metrics.

## Who It's For

Ritual is built for people who:

- Already self-track but feel overwhelmed by scattered data
- Want to build better habits without obsessing over them
- Care more about reflection and patterns than real-time dashboards
- Want a calm, daily review that fits into a routine
- Want their computer, phone, wearable, and manual logs to explain each other

## The Philosophy

<Info>
  Ritual collects data to serve reflection — not the other way around. Every feature is designed to make your daily review faster and more useful, not to maximize engagement.
</Info>

One day everyone will routinely collect data about themselves and their behavior. Ritual is built to be the layer that makes that data actually useful — treating habits, learning, time, and trade-offs as native primitives, not afterthoughts.
