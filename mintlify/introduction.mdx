Ritual is a collection of self-tracking and observability tools used to measure and quantify your behavior.

## What is Ritual?

Ritual connects to the devices you use and wear every day.
You can connect your wearable devices (Apple Watch, Whoop, Oura Ring, Garmin), computer, phone, car (Tesla), and bank accounts (Plaid) to generate logs that update automatically — or you can manually submit logs in natural language or voice mode. Chat lets you quickly log habits and ask direct questions about your data.

## Who is Ritual for?

Ritual is built for anyone that wants deeper observability of their life. It is built for running experiments, finding behavioral patterns, and most importantly; self knowledge. Ritual is currently being used by students, athletes, biohackers, researchers, and engineers.

## Current Features

- **Index:** Single source of truth of your behavioral data
- **Logs:** Searchable records and traces of events with virtualized infinite-scroll tables
- **Calendar:** Plan tasks and visualize your daily/weekly/monthly schedule
- **Integrations:** Connect your computer, phone, wearable devices, car, and bank accounts
- **Chat:** Log habits in plain language and ask simple questions about your data
- **Context Understanding:** Combines habit logs, wearable data, phone usage, location context, and structured computer activity
- **Analytics:** Habit statistics, trend analysis, correlation matrices, streak tracking, and anomaly detection
- **Data Import:** Bulk import from CSV, screenshots, Apple Health, Whoop, Oura, Garmin, and Fitbit with validation, conflict resolution, and undo support
- **Ritual Watcher:** Tracks app and website usage locally, then syncs Computer Time into your metrics
