Ritual is a collection of self-tracking tools and integrations used to measure and quantify your behavior.

## What is Ritual?

Ritual connects to the devices you use and wear every day.
You can connect your wearable devices (Apple Watch, Whoop, Oura Ring) computer, and phone to generate logs that update automatically or you can manually submit logs in natural language or voice mode. AI and vector search can be used to make sense of your behavioral data, parsing it to find new insights and patterns.

## Who is Ritual for?

Ritual is built for anyone that wants deeper observability of their life. It is built for anyone that wants to improve, because you will never get better if the progress of what you are working on is impossible to measure.

## Current Features

- **Index:** single source of truth of your behavioral data
- **Metrics:** charts and analytics to visualize changes over time
- **Logs:** searchable records and traces of events
- **Calendar:** Plan tasks and visualize your daily/weekly/monthly schedule
- **Integrations:** Connect your computer, phone, and wearable devices
- **Context Understanding:** Combine Ritual Watcher, Ritual Recorder, OCR, and memory search to reconstruct what you were doing
- **Data Import:** Bulk import data in .csv, .xml, .json, .png, or .pdf formats
- **Ritual Watcher:** Tracks your app and browser usage statistics
- **Ritual Recorder:** Records your screen and converts frames to OCR text
- **AI Search:** Ask questions and chat with your behavioral data using specific tool calls.
- **Vector Search:** Search over screen recording data that's been chunked and converted into embeddings.
