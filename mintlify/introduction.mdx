Ritual helps you measure daily life in a way that stays useful, calm, and practical.

Instead of collecting data for its own sake, Ritual focuses on reflection, patterns, and better decisions. It combines quick manual logging with passive signals from your Mac, iPhone, wearables, and context sources so your daily review has a fuller picture of what happened.

## Why Ritual?

Most tracking tools are built to optimize a single metric. Ritual is built to help you understand your life as a whole — the interplay between sleep, habits, focus, and time.

## What Ritual Tracks

Ritual can track:

- **Manual habits** — natural-language and voice logs such as “worked out for 1 hour” or “had 80mg caffeine.”
- **Computer activity** — Mac app and website usage through the desktop watcher.
- **iPhone Time** — iPhone foreground app usage from Apple Biome data when the required local sync data is available.
- **Wearables and health signals** — sleep, steps, workouts, heart rate, recovery, and related biometric context.
- **Location context** — optional location enrichment for habit and activity logs.

The app then turns those inputs into Overview totals, Metrics charts, Logs rows, Calendar context, and AI summaries.
