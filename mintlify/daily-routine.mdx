---
title: "Daily Routine"
description: "A simple morning, midday, and evening rhythm for getting the most out of Ritual."
---

Ritual is designed around a simple daily rhythm. You don't need to check it constantly — three short touchpoints are enough to get real value.

## Morning

**Check the Index and set intent for the day.**

- Review yesterday's habit completion and any trends
- See what your integrations captured overnight, such as sleep, recovery, steps, heart rate, and iPhone Time
- Note one or two priorities for today

This should take 3–5 minutes.

## Midday

**Use quick logs and timer sessions to stay consistent.**

- Log habits in natural language or voice as you complete them
- Let Computer Time track app and website usage in the background
- Make any quick notes or context entries that feel relevant

This is the highest-friction moment — keep it as lightweight as possible.

## Evening

**Review trends and ask chat for a short summary.**

- Open the Logs view to see how today stacked up
- Check Metrics for habit trends, app usage, website usage, and iPhone Time
- Ask the chat assistant for a quick daily summary
- Note anything worth remembering for tomorrow

This should take 5 minutes.

---

<Tip>
  The most important thing isn't perfect tracking — it's consistent tracking. A 60-second evening review every day is worth more than a two-hour deep dive once a week.
</Tip>
