---
title: "Daily Routine"
description: "A practical morning, midday, and evening rhythm for getting the most out of Ritual."
---

Ritual is designed around a lightweight daily rhythm. Three short touchpoints are enough to get real value — you don't need to check it constantly.

## Morning (5–10 minutes)

**Open the Index view and review yesterday.**

- Scan your habit completion from the day before
- Check your Whoop recovery score and sleep duration if connected
- See your computer activity summary — which apps you used most
- Ask the AI assistant for a quick summary if you want a narrative view

The Index view surfaces your most important signals automatically. You're not searching for data — it's already organized for you.

## Midday (30 seconds per log)

**Log habits as you complete them.**

Ritual is designed for fast logging. A single habit entry takes under 5 seconds from the native widget. A few things that help:

- Use the native menubar widget to log without opening the full app
- Start a Timer session when doing focused work — it logs automatically when you stop
- Computer time is tracked passively — no logging needed

<Tip>
  The most friction-reducing change you can make: add the Ritual widget to your menubar so logging requires zero app switching.
</Tip>

## Evening (5 minutes)

**Review today and ask the assistant anything.**

- Open the Logs view to scan today's entries
- Check the Calendar view to see how today fits into your recent pattern
- Ask the AI assistant: *"How did today go compared to my average?"*

This is where patterns become visible. A single day's data tells you almost nothing. A week of consistent review starts to show you what's actually working.

---

## What consistency actually looks like

The goal isn't perfect logging — it's consistent enough logging that patterns emerge. Some benchmarks:

| Frequency | What you get |
|---|---|
| Daily review | Immediate feedback on yesterday |
| Weekly consistency | Habit streaks and drops become visible |
| Monthly data | Correlation analysis becomes meaningful |
| 3+ months | The AI assistant can answer nuanced pattern questions |

<Info>
  Ritual becomes significantly more useful after 30 days of consistent use. The AI assistant's pattern analysis improves as your data history grows.
</Info>
