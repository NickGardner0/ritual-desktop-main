Ritual is organized around a set of views, each designed for a specific part of your day.

## Views

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Index" icon="house">
    Overview of your day, all-time totals, selected date ranges, and current momentum. The starting point for every daily review.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Metrics" icon="chart-line">
    Charts, sparklines, habit rankings, app and website breakdowns, Computer Time, and iPhone Time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Logs" icon="list">
    Clean history view for reviewing habit entries, read-only daily rollups, quick edits, and consistency across habits.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Calendar" icon="calendar">
    Connect behavior with time patterns. See how your habits cluster around days of the week, seasons, trips, and life events.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Chat" icon="message">
    Built-in AI assistant for plain-language logging, summaries, and reflections. Ask questions about your data and get grounded answers.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Reports" icon="file-text">
    Longer-form readouts and summaries for reviewing patterns over weeks and months.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Tools

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Natural-Language Logging" icon="message-plus">
    Type or speak what happened. Ritual parses the habit, value, unit, and date, then updates your metrics.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Timer Sessions" icon="timer">
    Focused work blocks that automatically feed into your habit history. Log deep work without extra steps.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Desktop Watcher" icon="monitor">
    Local Mac activity tracking for Computer Time, app rankings, website rankings, and recent activity context.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Location Context" icon="map-pin">
    Optional place context can be attached to habit logs and activity events when location pings are available.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Onboarding" icon="wand-magic-sparkles">
    Simple onboarding that personalizes your starting setup based on what you actually want to track.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Design Principles

Every feature in Ritual is built around three rules:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fast to log">
    Capturing a habit or note should take under three seconds. If it's slower than that, you won't do it consistently.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Easy to review">
    Every view is designed so you can scan your data quickly and get back to your day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Useful over time">
    The goal is patterns, not single data points. Ritual becomes more valuable the longer you use it.
  </Step>
</Steps>
