Ritual is organized around a set of views, each designed for a specific part of your day.

## Views

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Index" icon="house">
    Overview of your day, your trends, and your current momentum. The starting point for every daily review.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Logs" icon="list">
    Clean history view for reviewing past entries, making quick edits, and checking consistency across habits.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Calendar" icon="calendar">
    Connect behavior with actual time patterns. See how your habits cluster around days of the week, seasons, or life events.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Chat" icon="message">
    Built-in AI assistant for plain-language summaries and reflections. Ask questions about your data and get real answers.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Tools

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Timer Sessions" icon="timer">
    Focused work blocks that automatically feed into your habit history. Log deep work without extra steps.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Onboarding" icon="wand-magic-sparkles">
    Simple onboarding that personalizes your starting setup based on what you actually want to track.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Design Principles

Every feature in Ritual is built around three rules:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fast to log">
    Capturing a habit or note should take under three seconds. If it's slower than that, you won't do it consistently.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Easy to review">
    Every view is designed so you can scan your data quickly and get back to your day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Useful over time">
    The goal is patterns, not single data points. Ritual becomes more valuable the longer you use it.
  </Step>
</Steps>
