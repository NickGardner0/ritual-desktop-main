Ritual follows a simple four-step loop that runs every day.

## The Loop

<Steps>
  <Step title="Capture">
    Ritual pulls in signals from habits, integrations, and your day-to-day routine. Some arrive automatically, such as computer activity, wearable data, iPhone Time, and location context. Others are logged manually in seconds through natural language or voice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Organize">
    Data is normalized into daily facts and read models for Overview, Metrics, Logs, and Calendar. You don't have to rebuild your day from scattered sources — Ritual surfaces the same canonical totals everywhere.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Spot Patterns">
    Use visual views and the built-in chat to identify what's actually going on. Ask plain-language questions about habits, app usage, sleep, biometrics, and calendar context.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust">
    Make small, measurable changes to your routine. Ritual makes it easy to see what's working and what isn't over time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The Daily Review

The daily review is the core ritual. It takes 5–10 minutes and gives you:

- A snapshot of yesterday's habits and context
- Your current trends across key areas
- A short summary from the AI assistant
- App, website, iPhone, wearable, and location context when available

The goal isn't to spend more time analyzing — it's to spend less time guessing.
