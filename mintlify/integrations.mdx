---
title: "Integrations"
description: "Devices and data sources that connect to Ritual automatically."
---

Ritual works best when manual tracking and passive sources are combined. Passive integrations reduce friction — you don't have to remember to log what your devices already know.

## Live Integrations

These are available now in the current version of Ritual.

| Source | Type | Notes |
|--------|------|-------|
| Whoop | Wearable | Sleep, recovery, and strain data |
| Apple Watch | Wearable | Activity, heart rate via iOS companion app |
| Computer Tracking | Desktop | Active app and focus session tracking |
| Weather | Context | Daily weather automatically added to your log |

## Planned Integrations

These are in development and will be available in future releases.

| Source | Type |
|--------|------|
| Apple Screen Time | Device usage |
| Oura Ring | Wearable |
| Fitbit | Wearable |
| Garmin | Wearable |

<Info>
  Passive integrations don't replace manual habits — they add context. Seeing that you slept 5 hours and still hit your workout is more useful than either data point alone.
</Info>

## Philosophy

Ritual treats integrations as context layers, not scorecards. The goal is to give you a richer picture of why your habits went the way they did, not to add more things to optimize.
